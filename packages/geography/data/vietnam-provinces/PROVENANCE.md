# Vietnam provincial-level divisions

This dataset represents Vietnam's post-2025 structure of 34 provincial-level administrative units: 28 provinces and six centrally governed cities. The structure and official names follow Decision 19/2025/QD-TTg, effective 1 July 2025, as published by the Government of Vietnam.

Boundary source: the preprocessed 34-unit `public/provinces.json` from `lamngockhuong/vietnam-3d-map`, whose documented source is the Vietnam Department of Survey, Mapping and Geographic Information's `sapnhap.bando.com.vn` map service. The retained source snapshot was downloaded on 2 October 2026.

The WhackSmacker renderer changes colours, simplifies the presentation, and omits remote offshore islands from the learning view so the mainland provincial boundaries remain legible in a terminal. Administrative boundaries are educational reference material, not a boundary survey.

2026-10-03 artwork revision: the nationwide and regional numbered, named, neutral-reference, and highlighted-division assets render directly on the cream artwork canvas without the former pale-blue framed panel. The map extents and artwork placement were tightened to maximize each complete visible landmass while retaining the established callouts and legends. North and South use landscape artwork; Central uses portrait artwork to match its long north-south shape. Every regional reference and question/answer asset was regenerated from the same regional geometry and layout rules.

2026-10-03 regional scale revision: North, Central, and South now follow the canonical Wandering the World priority of maximizing complete landmass size first and readable label size second. Regional map margins were tightened, map areas enlarged, keys moved farther right, and regional number/key fonts enlarged. Crowded units retain full-size external labels and leader lines.
