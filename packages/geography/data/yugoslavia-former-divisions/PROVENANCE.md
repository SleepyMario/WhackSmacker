# Yugoslavia (Former) map provenance

This historical learning view shows the six constituent republics of the Socialist Federal Republic of Yugoslavia: Slovenia, Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Serbia, Montenegro and Macedonia. Modern open geoBoundaries ADM0 geometry was downloaded on 3 October 2026. Kosovo is combined with Serbia because it was an autonomous province within the Socialist Republic of Serbia rather than a constituent republic. The map is a historical teaching aid, not a statement about present political status or exact historical boundary surveying.
