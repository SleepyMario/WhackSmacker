# Canada map provenance

- Dataset: Natural Earth `Admin 1 – States, Provinces` at 1:10m scale
- Represented divisions: 10 provinces and 3 territories
- Source: Natural Earth
- License: Public domain
- Downloaded: 4 October 2026
- Source: <https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson>

WhackSmacker projects the source geometry with a Canada-focused Lambert conformal conic projection and generates the learner colours, numbered and named reference maps, deliberately positioned labels, readable callouts, and highlighted question/answer artwork.
