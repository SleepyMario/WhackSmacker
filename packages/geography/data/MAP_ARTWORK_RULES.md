# Wandering the World map artwork rules

These rules apply to every map in Wandering the World.

1. **Maximize the landmass first.** Use as much of the artwork canvas as practical while keeping the complete studied landmass, islands, borders, and a small visible safety margin inside the image.
2. **Maximize readable text second.** After the landmass has been maximized, use the largest practical font for numbers, names, and keys.
3. Labels may be placed inside their areas when that is clear. Small or crowded areas may use labels in open canvas space with leader lines.
4. A key or external label column must move toward the canvas edge instead of needlessly shrinking the map.
5. Choose portrait, landscape, or top-down paired artwork from the shape of the studied geography. Do not force every map into one fixed arrangement.
6. Never crop a studied province, prefecture, state, region, island, or equivalent unit merely to enlarge the rest of the map.
7. Keep all text, lines, and mapped territory fully inside the artwork.

Priority conflicts are resolved in this order: complete geography, maximum landmass size, maximum readable font size, then decorative spacing.
