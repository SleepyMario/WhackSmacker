# Continents - Easy numbered map

Source: Natural Earth 1:110m land and admin-0 data, public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson

Adapted locally on 2026-09-14 for Wandering the World, Mollweide projection.
Approved numbering: 1 North America; 2 South America; 3 Africa; 4 Eurasia; 5 Oceania; 6 Antarctica.
Europe and Asia combined. French Guiana assigned to South America, Hawaii and western New Guinea to Oceania. Simplified overview; small islands and transcontinental boundaries are generalized.
PNG is the user-reviewed numbered reference. Source reference and numbered SVG/PDF retained in the authoring workspace; recovery publication awaits user review and commit approval.
