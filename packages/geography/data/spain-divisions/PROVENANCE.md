WhackSmacker generates the learner colours, numbered and named reference maps, readable callouts or insets, and highlighted question/answer artwork.

The Canary Islands are retained as one complete autonomous community but displayed in a compact, labelled figure-level inset at the bottom left. Their internal island positions and relative scale are preserved. Because the inset does not participate in the main map's geographic extent, mainland Spain and the nearby Balearic Islands can occupy substantially more of the artwork. Ceuta and Melilla retain their geographic relationship in the primary map area. The same inset placement is used in every numbered, named, neutral-reference, highlighted-question and revealed-answer image.

A shallow section of the Moroccan coastline is drawn beneath southern Spain so that Ceuta and Melilla have readable geographic context. Gibraltar is also drawn at its real position as neutral context, without a label, number, answer option or deck entry. The Moroccan and Gibraltar outlines come from geoBoundaries `gbOpen` ADM0 sources and share the primary map's coordinate system: they are clipped by the artwork extent but are not stretched, rotated or repositioned.
