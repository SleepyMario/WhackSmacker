WhackSmacker generates the learner colours, numbered and named reference maps, readable callouts or insets, and highlighted question/answer artwork.

The Canary Islands are retained as one complete autonomous community but displayed in a compact figure-level inset at the bottom left. Their internal island positions and relative scale are preserved. Because the inset does not participate in the main map's geographic extent, mainland Spain and the nearby Balearic Islands can occupy substantially more of the artwork. The island shapes remain visible in the neutral reference, but its `Canary Islands` caption is omitted because that reference is displayed beside the active question in easy mode and the text would disclose the answer. The caption remains present in the numbered and named overviews and in the question/answer artwork.

Ceuta and Melilla retain their true positions and outlines in the primary map area. Two anonymous enlarged-detail panels repeat those outlines at readable scale so their selected-state fill can be seen in question artwork. The panels name a city only in its revealed answer and in the named overview; they do not print either name on questions or on the neutral reference.

A shallow section of the Moroccan coastline is drawn beneath southern Spain so that Ceuta and Melilla have readable geographic context. Gibraltar is also drawn at its real position as neutral context, without a label, number, answer option or deck entry. The Moroccan and Gibraltar outlines come from geoBoundaries `gbOpen` ADM0 sources and share the primary map's coordinate system: they are clipped by the artwork extent but are not stretched, rotated or repositioned.
