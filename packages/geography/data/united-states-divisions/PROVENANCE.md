# United States map provenance

- Dataset: Natural Earth `Admin 1 – States, Provinces` at 1:10m scale
- Represented divisions: the 50 states
- License: Public domain
- Downloaded: 4 October 2026
- Source: <https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson>

The District of Columbia is excluded because this deck covers the 50 states. WhackSmacker projects the contiguous states in a United States-focused conic view, places Alaska and Hawaii in conventional readable insets, and generates the learner colours, large numbered and named reference maps, readable callouts, and highlighted question/answer artwork. Alaska and Hawaii are not shown in their geographic positions or at the same scale as the contiguous states.
