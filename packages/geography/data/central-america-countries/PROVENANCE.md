# Central America countries and territories artwork provenance

The country and territory boundaries were derived from the Natural Earth 1:10m map-unit dataset already preserved with the North America source material. Bonaire, Saba, and Sint Eustatius were retained as distinct educational entries from Natural Earth administrative-level geometry.

WhackSmacker's renderer preserves the geographic shapes, aspect, and relative positions of all included land. It adds consistent colours, numbered labels, short non-crossing callouts for small islands, and orange highlights for quiz artwork. The scope is geographical and status-neutral: dependencies and constituent territories are labelled with the administering state in parentheses.
