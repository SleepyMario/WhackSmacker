# Italy region map provenance

The 20 constitutional regions are assembled from the public-domain Natural Earth 1:10m administrative-1 province geometry. Province polygons are grouped by their `region` field; learner labels use Italian region names. Downloaded 3 October 2026. Source: <https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector>.

The 3 October 2026 artwork revision omits the two southernmost tiny Sicilian source components, Lampedusa and Linosa, from the study-map rendering. Sicily remains one complete deck answer and the principal island of Sicily, Sardinia and the other retained islands remain in their true positions and proportions. Removing those two remote components and tightening the outer margins allows the studied Italian landmass to fill substantially more of every numbered, named, neutral-reference, highlighted-question and revealed-answer image. No retained geography is stretched, rotated or repositioned.
