# Russian Federation federal-district map provenance

- Base geometry: geoBoundaries ADM1 geometry for Russia and Ukraine
- geoBoundaries license: CC BY 4.0
- Source: <https://www.geoboundaries.org/>
- District membership and ordering: Federal State Statistics Service (Rosstat)
- Rosstat source: <https://rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/Region_Subekt_2025.htm>
- Retrieved: 4 October 2026

The source geometry was grouped into the eight federal districts used by
Rosstat: Central, Northwestern, Southern, North Caucasian, Volga, Ural,
Siberian, and Far Eastern. Following Rosstat's current classifier arrangement,
Donetsk, Luhansk, Zaporozhye, and Kherson are grouped provisionally with the
Southern Federal District. WhackSmacker generates the learner colours,
numbered and named reference maps, and highlighted question and answer
artwork. The named map uses concise district labels because the deck title
already identifies them as federal districts.
