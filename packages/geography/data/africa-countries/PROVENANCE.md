# Africa countries and territories artwork provenance

The boundaries are derived from Natural Earth’s public-domain **Admin 0 —
Countries** and **Admin 0 — Map Units** datasets at 1:10m scale, version 5.1.1.
The retained source snapshot supplies the African land and island geometry used
by the 59 learner entries.

Source pages:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-details/

Upstream repository snapshots:
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_map_units.geojson

The deck covers the 54 generally recognized sovereign African states together
with Western Sahara, Somaliland, Mayotte (France), Réunion (France), and Saint
Helena (United Kingdom). Bir Tawil is not a learner entry because it is not
claimed as a state or territory by either neighbouring country.

WhackSmacker preserves the source geometry and geographic proportions. Numbers,
straight leader lines, and translucent island locator circles are learner-facing
additions. Country and territory names deliberately do not appear in the
artwork; they remain in prompts, choices, typed answers, and textual feedback.
The tall map uses the side-by-side card layout so that its landmass remains as
large and legible as possible.
