WhackSmacker generates the learner colours, numbered and named reference maps, readable callouts or insets, and highlighted question/answer artwork.

The 3 October 2026 artwork revision removes the remote-island distortion from the primary map extent. Taiwan and its nearby eastern islands now fill the main artwork, while Lienchiang County, Kinmen County, Penghu County and the Dongsha component of Kaohsiung City appear in four stable, labelled figure-level insets. Every numbered, named, neutral-reference, highlighted-question and revealed-answer image uses the same arrangement. Island shapes and internal proportions are preserved; the insets change display scale only. Highlighting Kaohsiung City also highlights Dongsha in its inset.

Learner labels use current official-style English administrative names, including the still-separate Hsinchu City/Hsinchu County and Chiayi City/Chiayi County pairs. Municipalities and provincial cities carry `City`; the three remote county jurisdictions use Lienchiang County, Kinmen County and Penghu County. Existing card IDs and asset stems were retained through this naming correction so saved review progress remains attached to the same administrative divisions.
