# USSR (Former) map provenance

This historical learning view shows the 15 union republics present at the end of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics. It uses the complete Soviet-era English republic names rather than abbreviations.

- Geometry basis: Natural Earth `Admin 0 – Countries` at 1:10m scale
- Historical scope: the 15 union republics at the dissolution of the USSR in 1991
- License: Public domain
- Downloaded: 4 October 2026
- Source: <https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/master/geojson>

Modern national geometry is used as a practical approximation of the former inter-republic boundaries. The map is a historical teaching aid rather than a statement about present political status or exact historical boundary surveying. WhackSmacker projects the geometry in a Eurasia-centred Lambert conformal conic view and generates the learner colours, large numbered and named reference maps, readable callouts, and highlighted question/answer artwork.

Crimea is assigned directly to the Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, reflecting its constitutional location within the USSR in 1991. No contemporary disputed-territory treatment is used in this historical deck.
