# Belgium region map provenance

- Source geometry: the retained 2024 Belgian NUTS 2 source used by the `Belgium — Provinces and Brussels` deck
- Source file: `../belgium-provinces/source-BEL-nuts2-2024.geojson`
- Derived grouping: the five Flemish provinces form Flanders, the five Walloon provinces form Wallonia, and Brussels remains its own region
- Learner-facing names: Flanders, Brussels, Wallonia

WhackSmacker dissolves the province-level presentation into Belgium's three administrative regions. Province borders inside Flanders and Wallonia are suppressed; only the country outline and boundaries between the three regions remain. Brussels uses an external label and leader line so it remains readable without shrinking the Belgian landmass.
