# Europe countries and territories artwork provenance

The boundaries are derived from Natural Earth’s public-domain **Admin 0 —
Countries** dataset at 1:10m scale, version 5.1.1. The retained source snapshot
contains the 51 features classified by Natural Earth in Europe. This includes
Kosovo and the separately represented Faroe Islands, Gibraltar, Guernsey,
Jersey, Isle of Man, and Åland.

Source page:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/

Upstream repository snapshot:
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson

WhackSmacker preserves the source geometry and geographic proportions. The
Europe canvas uses a conventional Europe-centred extent; Russia is shown through
its European portion so that the remainder of Europe stays legible. Numbers are
learner-facing additions. Country names deliberately do not appear anywhere in
the artwork: they remain in prompts, choices, typed answers, and textual
feedback. Small countries and territories use straight callouts into open water
or unused canvas.
