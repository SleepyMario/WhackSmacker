# Russian Federation federal-subject map provenance

- Base geometry: geoBoundaries ADM1 geometry for Russia and Ukraine
- geoBoundaries license: CC BY 4.0
- Source: <https://www.geoboundaries.org/>
- Federal-subject grouping and ordering: Federal State Statistics Service (Rosstat)
- Rosstat source: <https://rosstat.gov.ru/storage/mediabank/Region_Subekt_2025.htm>
- Retrieved: 4 October 2026

This deck follows its explicitly selected 89-subject Russian Federation scope.
The numbered overview places all 89 subjects in one stable sequence, grouped by
federal district. The complete country appears above four enlarged western
panels: Central; Northwestern; Southern and North Caucasian; and Volga and
Ural. Siberian and Far Eastern subjects retain their numbers on the nationwide
map. The enlarged panels and individually reviewed leader lines keep compact
subjects recognizable without reducing the common number size.

The artwork intentionally contains no 89-name legend. The numbered overview,
neutral references, highlighted questions, and revealed answers all derive
from the same geometry, ordering, extents, panel layout, and regional captions.
The full subject names remain in deck metadata and learner-facing prompts.
