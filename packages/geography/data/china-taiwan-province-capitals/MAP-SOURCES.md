# China (Taiwan) province-capital map sources

The first-level outlines follow the validated 22-division China (Taiwan)
teaching inventory in `packages/geography/data/china-roc-divisions`.
Lower-level boundaries come from geoBoundaries release `TWN-ADM2-52511910`
(represented year 2017), derived from OpenStreetMap/Wambacher data under
CC BY-SA 2.0.

For an ordinary county, the county seat's township or city is coloured inside
the first-level outline. A first-level city is its own administrative seat, so
its complete first-level outline is coloured while internal boundaries remain
visible. Every lower-level boundary is hard-clipped to the first-level outline.
