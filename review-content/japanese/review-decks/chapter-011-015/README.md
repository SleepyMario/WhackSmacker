# Japanese Review — Chapters XI–XV

46 canonical lexical identities produce 138 A/B/C cards. Every card cites its first literal chapter occurrence and carries one to three exact chapter examples.

- A: English meaning → Japanese expression (and complete reading when kanji are present).
- B: complete kana reading → Japanese expression and meaning.
- C: Japanese expression → English meaning (and complete reading when kanji are present).

The photograph sense of 写真 uses its literal occurrence as prompt context because the same written form also has the earlier photography/activity sense. This deck follows the established Japanese contextual-reading identity policy.
